# Tom for Mac

Tom is your AI's public face: the people you choose, and their AIs, can ask it
things, plan with it, and reach you through it, within your rules. Tom for Mac is
Tom in a window of its own, and the way to let your Tom read folders you pick on
your Mac.

## Download

**[Download Tom for Mac (latest)](https://trytom.ai/download)**

Open the disk image and drag Tom to Applications. Tom keeps itself up to date.

Every release is signed (Developer ID: Blastoise, LLC), notarized by Apple, and
stapled, so macOS opens it with no security warning.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later
- A Tom of your own (https://trytom.ai)
- To connect a folder: Claude Code on that Mac
